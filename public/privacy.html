Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Football Coach High School Edition (the "game") is made by Facada Inc ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you play at footballcoachgame.com, why, and the choices you have. You can play the whole game without an account.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Your saved games and settingsAlways, stored in your browserSo you can keep playing. Without an account they never leave your device.
Hall of Fame entries: your coach name, school and career resultsWhen you finish a scored seasonTo rank careers on the public Hall of Fame
Name, email address and the sign-in service you used (Google, Facebook or an email link)Only if you create an accountTo sign you in and recognize you across devices
Your saved games, copied to your accountOnly when signed inCloud saves: your careers on every device
Your ducats: each purchase (the pack, the price, when) and what you spent them onOnly if you buy ducatsTo keep your balance and handle refunds. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us.
Whether you want game news by email, and when you said soOnly if you tick the boxTo send news only to players who asked for it
How the game is used (pages and screens, game actions such as games played, seasons and recruiting choices, device type, approximate location)Only if you allow analytics cookiesTo improve the game (Google Analytics)

We don't collect passwords: Google and Facebook sign you in, or we email you a one-time link.

Cookies and local storage

  • Necessary: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in (for 30 days), and a short-lived one while a sign-in is in progress. Your saves and settings are kept in your browser's storage.
  • Analytics (optional): Google Analytics cookies, only after you choose "Allow analytics". You can change your choice any time in Settings.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We use these services to run the game, and they process data for us:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, accounts, saves and the Hall of Fame
  • Google and Meta (Facebook): signing in, if you choose them
  • Resend: sending sign-in emails (and game news if you opted in)
  • Stripe: ducat payments, refunds and receipts, if you buy ducats
  • Google Analytics: usage statistics, only with your consent

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Email

Sign-in links are sent only when you ask for one. Game news is sent only if you ticked "Send me game news", and every news email has an unsubscribe link. You can also turn news off in Settings, Account.

How long we keep it

Account information, cloud saves and your ducat history are kept until you delete your account; Stripe keeps its own payment records as the law requires. Sign-in links expire after 15 minutes. Hall of Fame entries stay on the public board. Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics settings.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and everything stored with it in Settings, Account, or by emailing us.
  • Access or correct your information by emailing us.
  • Stop game news with the unsubscribe link or in Settings.
  • Change cookies in Settings, Cookies.
  • If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove the game from your Facebook settings; we then delete your account information.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights, such as asking for a copy of your information or objecting to its use. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens and email links are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and for important changes we'll tell signed-in players in the game.
