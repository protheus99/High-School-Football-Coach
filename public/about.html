The high school football coaching sim. Take over a program and build it into a dynasty. Recruit and develop players, call the plays on Friday night, and chase a state and national title, season after season.

▶ Play now Free, right in your browser

Features

🏈Game day

  • Play every game live and call the plays, or sim the ones you'd rather skip
  • Make the big calls: 4th downs, the red zone, the try after a touchdown
  • Halftime talks that change the second half
  • Player of the Game and a post-game press conference
  • A game counts once it kicks off: no reloading to replay a loss

🧢Your program

  • Real high schools and districts from football states across the U.S.
  • Three ways to start: Reclaiming the Crown, Powerhouses, or Your Pick
  • Hire a coaching staff, graded D to S, and unlock coach talents
  • Weekly practice that builds players for good, with fatigue and injury risk
  • Depth chart, ratings and potential grades for every player

🔍Recruiting

  • Build your pipeline of middle schoolers with texts, calls and home visits
  • 14 programs, from the Youth Football Clinic to Lockdown League and War Zone
  • Chase State and National prospects who want to win, start early or stay home
  • Signing day: see who joined and who chose a rival
  • Help your seniors earn college offers

🏆Seasons and legacy

  • The full year: spring, summer, training camp, district play and real state playoff brackets
  • The rest of the country plays the same season, with a computer national Top 25
  • Mr. Football, All-State teams and the season banquet
  • Tough calls: dilemmas, boosters and compliance investigations
  • Contracts, board reviews and job offers from other programs
  • An online Hall of Fame for careers, and cloud saves when you sign in

How to play

  1. 1
    New GamePick a state and a program, name your coach and choose your contract.
  2. 2
    Off-season and pre-seasonHire your staff, run recruiting programs, set the depth chart, and push practice in the 2× training weeks.
  3. 3
    Regular seasonEach week, set practice and answer what comes up, then play your game live or sim it.
  4. 4
    PlayoffsWin your district, survive the state bracket, and watch the national poll.
  5. 5
    Banquet and beyondAwards and college signings for your seniors; newcomers join on signing day, and the board reviews your contract.

Tips

  • 📈Practice gains are permanent; fatigue and injury risk only last the week. Go Full in the 2× weeks.
  • 🎯A prospect is evaluated at 50 interest and commits at 80.
  • 🏠Your own district is the easiest to recruit; out-of-state prospects are the hardest.
  • ⚖️Shady dilemma choices pay big Coach Points, and can end in a playoff ban.
  • 🎓Get every senior a college offer: each signee counts toward your Hall of Fame career.
